How AIRGO works

Smart matching for airport trips

Our algorithm connects co-travelers with the same airport, flight window, and nearby pickup locations.

01
Passenger registers
Sign up with phone number, verify OTP, and set your home city.
02
Search for a ride
Enter pickup location, target airport, date and time. The app finds matches instantly.
03
Smart matching
Scores trips by airport (40%), flight time (30%), pickup distance (20%), and luggage (10%).
04
Book & pay
Pay securely via Paystack. Funds are held until trip completion, then released to the driver.
